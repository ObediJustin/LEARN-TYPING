# Third-party vocabulary notices

LEARN_TYPING redistributes adapted word selections. The application code remains separate from these datasets.

## FrequencyWords / OpenSubtitles 2018

Languages: French, English, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, German, Turkish, Indonesian, Dutch, Polish and Romanian.

- Source: [hermitdave/FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords)
- Original corpus: OpenSubtitles 2018 contributors
- FrequencyWords author: Hermit Dave
- Content license: [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
- Transformation: NFC normalization, lowercase conversion, letter-only filtering, length grouping, deduplication and unsuitable-term exclusion.

## Kiswahili vocabulary

Language: Kiswahili (`sw-TZ`).

- Source: [Wordtide Kiswahili word list](https://wordtide.app/dict/sw.txt)

## Quality scope

The included entries are sourced and mechanically validated, but they have not received a complete independent linguist review. They are intended for typing practice, not as definitions, translations or a comprehensive language-learning curriculum.
